# Implementation status — 2026-07-28

This update records evidence after the Nix/mise setup, the portable M3/M4
completion pass, and the first direct Metal fast-path probe.

## Directly proven

- `XDG_CACHE_HOME="$PWD/.cache" mise run check` passes the tooling contract,
  formatting, shell checks, a fresh Nix CMake configure/build, and all CTest
  cases (including the new authoring-tool checks).
- The CPU fast and 4D oracles, bounded-simplex projection, runtime/stub
  benchmark, manifest writer, and corruption detector pass seeded portable
  tests. Machine-readable evidence remains under `results/cpu/` and
  `results/benchmarks/`.
- `tetcage_cage_generate` creates a deterministic AABB-plus-center starter cage
  with a non-degenerate padding policy for planar inputs. The generated cage
  compiles through the canonical asset compiler.
- `tetcage_cage_quality` samples posed tetrahedra and reports condition,
  minimum edge, mirrors/near-singular frames, and affine-versus-barycentric
  residuals with explicit fallback reasons.
- On the Apple M1 Max, the direct Metal path creates and compacts one immutable
  triangle BLAS per occupied tetrahedron, builds a TLAS, compiles the runtime
  MSL ray-query kernel, and emits timing/memory/correctness fields.

## Measured partial result

The deterministic one-tet Metal probe (`--copies 1 --rays 128 --motion 0
--compact`) reports one exact-boundary miss and one near-tangent mismatch in the
first eight constructed rays. Interior/centroid rays match the CPU fast oracle;
the full run reports `misses: 1`, maximum position error approximately `0.05`,
and maximum source-attribute error approximately `0.0833`. This is direct
evidence of a remaining M5 boundary/grazing policy issue, not a completed GPU
correctness gate. The unverified sandbox CTest remains intentionally separate.

## Blocked or not yet proven

- Vulkan/NVIDIA and CUDA remain blocked by absent NVIDIA hardware, Vulkan
  runtime/SDK, and CUDA tooling.
- Scale crossover, GPU-generated descriptors, refit/periodic-rebuild study,
  GPU watertight/hybrid traversal, and production renderer integrations remain
  open roadmap gates.
- Full animation-quality evidence needs representative clips and a dense
  deformation reference; the new authoring analyzer intentionally reports that
  limitation instead of treating a procedural sample as proof.
- Unreal and Cycles source integrations are unavailable in this checkout.
- RenderMan public-API feasibility still requires its pinned capability study
- and licensed prototype. The local header gate now records Riley prototype and
  instance-transform APIs in `results/renderman-feasibility.json`, but no
  public-header evidence of direct AS ownership or custom intersection control;
  the decision is explicitly `no_go` until a licensed runtime prototype proves
  otherwise.

## Reproduction

```sh
XDG_CACHE_HOME="$PWD/.cache" mise run check
cmake --build --preset dev --target tetcage_metal_fast_path
./build/dev/tetcage_metal_fast_path build/dev/one-tet.tetcage \
  /tmp/tetcage-metal-one-debug.json --copies 1 --rays 128 --motion 0 --compact
```
